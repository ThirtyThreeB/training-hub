Medial epicondylitis rehab
Use this as a conservative elbow-maintenance block. Keep pain at 0–2/10 during the work and no worse the next morning. If symptoms climb or grip strength drops, reduce load and volume.

Best placement: after lifting, after rows/pulling, or on an off day. Do not turn this into a forearm workout.
Progression
Start with isometrics when irritated. Add slow eccentrics when daily pain is controlled. Add load only when you can complete all reps cleanly with no next-day flare. Increase dumbbell weight in small jumps, not by chasing fatigue.
Avoid while irritated
Heavy supinated curls, high-volume pull-ups, death-grip rows, heavy farmer carries, aggressive wrist curls, and long gripping finishers. For rows and deadlifts, use a normal grip and stop before elbow pain changes your mechanics.
